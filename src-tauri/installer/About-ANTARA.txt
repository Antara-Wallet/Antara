ANTARA
A Solana-primary multichain wallet with on-chain multisig vaults.

Privy supplies identity and embedded spending wallets. Spending wallets are
separate from vaults.

Paired vaults (Solana) are Squads 2-of-2 multisigs: your Privy wallet and the
ANTARA guardian, a Privy server wallet under a deny-by-default policy. The
guardian approves a release only to one of your own linked Solana wallets,
and only within any per-release or daily caps you set with the optional
Limits layer (ANTARA sets none). Paired vaults have no on-chain time lock.
Both keys are held by Privy. Paired vaults cannot be activated until the
guardian is configured on the API server. Earlier EVM paired Safes are shown read-only.

Independent-owner vaults (Squads on Solana, Safe on EVM networks) remain in
Settings. Each new independent vault requires multiple signatures. A Squads
vault may set an optional on-chain time lock (none by default); Safe has none.

The legacy local keystore can no longer sign, reveal its recovery phrase or
private keys, or import a private key in this build. Do not send funds to a
legacy keystore account or its stealth addresses: this build cannot spend
them.
USB, Bluetooth and location readings are contextual signals, not independent
cryptographic signers.

This is a developer preview. Funded acceptance testing is not complete.
Configure your providers and test with limited funds before relying on a
deployment. Review README.md and docs/SECURITY.md.
